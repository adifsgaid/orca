The rules being tested

  1. Nothing moves while the active account is under the line (the tightest of its 5-hour and weekly windows).
  2. Only accounts that are in rotation, signed in, readable, and under the line can be picked; the one with the most room left wins.
  3. The new account must have at least the margin more room than the current one, so two accounts near the line never trade places.
  4. Never go straight back to the account it just left until that account's window has reset.
  5. After any switch (automatic or by hand) it waits the cooldown before switching again, so your own pick is respected.
  6. At a hard limit (100%) the margin, cooldown and no-going-back rules are dropped: any account with room beats a stopped agent.
  7. When every account is over the line, it moves to whichever comes back soonest, but only if that is meaningfully sooner.
  8. When every account is out, it waits and names the first one back.
